Dental case study

23 extra appointments in 30 days

23

extra appointments booked by StarBot in the first 30 days, with no extra reception hours.

The problem

A busy clinic was missing after-hours enquiries. Patients who found the website in the evening hit a voicemail or a contact form, and many had booked elsewhere before reception could call back the next morning.

What we installed

StarBot was trained on the clinic's treatments, prices, opening hours and FAQs, then connected to its booking calendar. Anything clinical or complex was handed to the team for a callback.

The result

StarBot answered patient questions and booked consultations automatically, day and night. Reception started each morning with new bookings already in the diary instead of a backlog of messages to return.
